## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
The solution is FARMVAULT – a Smart Village-Level Agricultural Storage, Processing and Distribution System.

It works by establishing a shared agricultural facility in villages where farmers can:
1. Collect: Bring their harvested crops to a common centre.
2. Store: Preserve produce using suitable storage facilities.
3. Grade: Separate crops based on quality.
4. Process: Convert suitable surplus crops into products such as tomato pulp.
5. Distribute: Supply produce directly to retailers, restaurants and consumers through organized bulk transportation.
6. Track: Use a digital platform to monitor inventory, expenses, sales and farmer payments.

## Technical Approach
The system follows five major technical stages:

1. Smart Collection: Farmers' crops are digitally weighed, quality-graded and tracked using unique QR codes.

2. IoT-Based Monitoring: ESP32 and temperature-humidity sensors monitor storage conditions and generate alerts when necessary.

3. AI-Powered Crop Decisions: A Python-based intelligent system analyses crop quality, market demand, prices and storage costs to recommend fresh sale, storage or processing.

4. Smart Distribution: The platform combines buyer orders and optimizes transportation routes to reduce logistics expenses.

5. Digital Transparency: A centralized database maintains inventory, sales, expenses and farmer payment records.

Technology Stack: Python, Django, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, MySQL, ESP32, IoT sensors and Machine Learning.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility, Challenges and Strategies – FARMVAULT

1. Analysis of Feasibility
. Technical: Uses existing technologies like Python, Django, IoT sensors and AI.
. Economic: Reduces investment by partnering with existing warehouses and processing units.
. Operational: Can be managed through farmer cooperatives and FPOs.
. Scalability: Can start in one village and gradually expand to multiple villages.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks
. High initial infrastructure costs.
. Limited farmer awareness and adoption.
. Internet and electricity interruptions.
. Sensor failures and maintenance issues.
. Market price fluctuations and limited processing facilities.

3. Strategies for Overcoming Challenges
. Partner with existing storage and processing facilities.
. Conduct farmer awareness programmes and provide local-language support.
. Introduce offline access and backup monitoring systems.
. Perform regular sensor maintenance.
. Use market-price analysis to make profitable crop utilization decisions.
## Impact and Benefits
Impact and Benefits – FARMVAULT

1. Potential Impact on the Target Audience
. Farmers: Improved earning opportunities through reduced transportation costs and better crop utilization.
. Consumers: More efficient distribution and the potential for affordable agricultural products.
. Retailers and Buyers: Reliable bulk supply with better inventory management.
. Local Communities: Improved agricultural infrastructure and employment opportunities.
  
2. Benefits of the Solution
. Social Benefits: Strengthens farmers' confidence, transparency and cooperation.
. Economic Benefits: Reduces supply-chain expenses and improves farmers' net earnings.
. Environmental Benefits: Minimizes post-harvest wastage through proper storage and processing.
. Technological Benefits: Introduces AI-based crop decisions, IoT monitoring and digital inventory management.
. Long-term Benefits: Creates a sustainable and scalable village-level agricultural distribution model
